Join Us

Join the AES Lab

We believe that every problem has a solution — some are just waiting to be created or discovered.

We are building an interdisciplinary team of engineers and scientists dedicated to advancing electrochemical energy storage and sustainable energy technologies. We are committed to developing our members' scientific, technical, and communication skills for careers in academia and industry, while fostering a culture of curiosity, collaboration, integrity, creativity, and mutual respect.

We welcome motivated students and researchers from Materials Science, Chemical Engineering, Chemistry, Electrochemistry, Energy Engineering, and related fields. Candidates from other disciplines with relevant expertise are also encouraged to apply.

Undergraduate Students

Undergraduate students interested in gaining research experience in battery materials, electrochemistry, and energy storage are encouraged to contact the PI.

Research Assistants

Applicants with a BS/BE or MS degree interested in battery materials and electrochemical energy storage are welcome to apply. Outstanding candidates may also have opportunities to pursue graduate studies at VinUniversity.

Postdoctoral Researchers

We welcome postdocs interested in fundamental and applied research in advanced battery materials, electrochemical energy storage, and sustainable energy technologies. Please include selected publications and a one-page research proposal.

Research Collaborations

We welcome collaborations with academic institutions, research organizations, and industry partners — especially fundamental research, interdisciplinary projects, and industry–academia partnerships that translate discoveries into practice.

How to apply

Email the PI with your CV and a brief description of your research interests and motivation for joining AES Lab, using the subject line [AES Lab_Applying Position_Your Name]. Applications are generally reviewed at the beginning of each month. See also the CEI website for current openings.
